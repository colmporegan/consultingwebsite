The problem

You're not short on leads. You're losing the ones you have.

Every lead that waits is a lead going cold, and the first agent to call back usually wins the conversation. Slow follow-up doesn't just lose a lead, it hands a five-figure commission to whoever answered faster.

You pay for leads you never reach

Between portals, ads, and your website, you spend real money on leads. Studies of online property inquiries have found nearly half never get a response at all. Every one is money spent and wasted.

Speed decides who wins

The odds of even reaching a new lead fall sharply within the first few minutes. Yet the typical agent takes far longer, with response times measured in hours. By then the lead has moved on to someone quicker.

Repeat clients and referrals make up around 40% of a typical agent's business, yet most of that database sits untouched. That is earned business quietly closing for someone else.
