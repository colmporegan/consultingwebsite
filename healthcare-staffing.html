The problem

Your growth is capped by recruiter capacity, not demand.

Every day lost to admin is another day a role stays open, and another placement that goes to a faster competitor.

01

Recruiters are at full capacity

Your best recruiters are already maxed out. Every new role competes for the same limited hours, so placements plateau no matter how much demand you have.

02

You can't scale without hiring

The only obvious lever left is adding recruiters: slow, expensive, and risky. Growth ends up waiting on payroll instead of pipeline.

03

Wasted capacity is lost revenue

A large part of every recruiter's week goes to admin instead of selling. That is time not spent with candidates, and placements that quietly slip away.

The mechanism

The Placement OS™

The Placement OS is not another tool to log into. It is a system that runs the repetitive candidate and CRM admin in the background, so your recruiters spend their time on candidates and clients instead of paperwork. It is built around how your desks already work, so it fits your process rather than forcing a new one.
