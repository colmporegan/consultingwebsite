The problem

Your recruiters are drowning in manual work.

The average healthcare role takes 40–60 days to fill, and every day it sits open is lost margin. Most of that time isn't spent placing — it's spent screening, posting, and chasing. JobTarget puts the average at ~49 days.

Screening & ranking CVs by hand

Recruiters read and score every resume manually — the single biggest time sink, often 10–12 hours per role before a shortlist even exists.

Posting across 10+ job boards

Every role gets copied and posted to a dozen boards by hand, then monitored separately. Hours gone before a single qualified candidate lands in the pipeline.

Scheduling chaos & cold candidates

Interview coordination, reschedules, and manual status updates keep good candidates waiting. They drop off, ghost, or take another offer — and the req stays open.

The solution

Introducing The Shortlist OS.

A done-for-you automation system that turns a stack of applicants into a ranked, interview-ready shortlist — in hours, not days. It sources and screens continuously, ranks candidates against your roles, distributes postings, and schedules interviews, so your team spends its time placing, not processing.
