EU AI Act & responsible adoption

Move forward with AI.
Keep oversight in place.

I help life science recruitment teams navigate the EU AI Act and turn regulatory considerations into practical decisions about how AI is selected, used and monitored.

AI used to analyse or filter applications, or evaluate candidates, can fall within the Act’s high-risk category. The intended use matters: a scheduling workflow and a candidate evaluation system need different assessments.

Read the European Commission’s AI Act overview ↗

Understand your use cases

Map where AI enters your process, assess risk classification and clarify responsibilities with your legal and compliance advisers.

Build in human oversight

Define review points, escalation routes and who can override an output. Keep recruiters accountable for candidate decisions.

Put governance into practice

Establish usage policies, staff training, documentation and checks for accuracy and bias. Review candidate data handling and GDPR considerations alongside AI risks.

Practical implementation and governance support; legal interpretation and formal compliance advice remain with your legal advisers.

Your questions, answered

Before we get started

Who do you work with?

Life science recruitment agencies and specialist recruitment teams working across biotech, pharma, medtech and clinical research, particularly teams held back by repetitive admin.

Can you work with our existing ATS or CRM?

We start by reviewing your current setup. Integration depends on the access and capabilities your systems provide. I’ll establish what’s feasible before recommending a build.

Will AI make hiring decisions?

The workflows I design keep recruiters responsible for candidate decisions. AI can prepare and organise information for review. Human review alone does not remove any applicable regulatory obligations.

Can we start with just one workflow?

Yes. A focused pilot lets us test a useful improvement, measure time saved and check quality before extending automation across your process.

How do you approach the EU AI Act?

We assess the intended use, identify governance needs and establish practical controls, working with your legal or compliance advisers where needed. No tool or human checkpoint is a blanket guarantee of compliance.

What happens on the discovery call?

We spend 30 minutes discussing your recruitment process, the work taking up your team’s time and where automation could help. You’ll leave with a clearer next step.
